How the women's picks work, and how they tested

NCAA Division I women's basketball. Experimental, first season. Updated November 2026.

Short version: past betting lines show a big edge, about 60-68% winners. That is far too big to be real money. Most past women's lines came from one soft book per game, often with low limits. This season's closing lines show no edge so far. The picks are posted as a paper test.

The model

  • Every D1 women's game since 2016 is pulled from ESPN: scores, box scores and player minutes.
  • Our own opponent-adjusted ratings are refit every day from games played before that day. They cover offense, defense and pace, and the four factors: shooting, turnovers, rebounding and free throws.
  • Gradient-boosted trees then project each game's margin and total. Inputs are the ratings, season-to-date stats, last season's ratings, player values (so returning players and transfers carry over), rest, travel and home court.
  • The model never sees the betting line.

How it was tested

The test is walk-forward. Each season is predicted by a model trained only on earlier seasons, then graded against the lines ESPN recorded from real sportsbooks. Line coverage is thin: about 88% of games in 2022-23, about half in 2023-24, and only 16-22% since.

The betting rules were chosen on one season, 2022-23 (the best covered), and then left alone:

  • Spreads: the model must differ from the line by more than 6 points.
  • Totals: the same 6-point gap, in November and December only.

Every other season was a check, not a tuning step. Break-even at -110 is 52.4%.

Why we don't trust the big numbers

  • Soft, single-book lines. Most past games had one book's number. A "closing" line may be a price that was pulled before tip, or one with a tiny limit.
  • The model is only about as accurate as those lines. Its average miss is about 9.7 points, the same as the line's. When two forecasts are equally good and disagree by 6+ points, the truth often lands between them. That alone produces hit rates near 60%.
  • Which games have lines. In thin seasons, books mostly list bigger programs and lopsided games. This November most lined games had spreads of 30+ points.
  • This season is the real test. At DraftKings, lines have moved toward the model after opening (about 3 points on average), but by tip the edge has been gone. Any edge is probably only in early lines and small limits.

Timing

Most women's lines post on game day. The model checks several times each morning and once the evening before. Each pick is frozen at the first line it saw, and its result is graded against that line.

Experimental and for fun. Not betting advice. No real money is placed on these picks.
